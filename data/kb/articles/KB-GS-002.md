# Building your first workflow

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 4.x.

## Steps

- Open Workflows and click New workflow.
- Pick a trigger (schedule, webhook or app event).
- Add steps with the + button and map fields from earlier steps.
- Click Test run, then Publish.

## Limits

- A workflow can have up to 50 steps.
- Each step has a default timeout of 60 seconds, configurable up to 300 seconds.
