# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 3.x and 4.x.

## Upgrade

- Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated.

## Downgrade

- Downgrades take effect at the next billing period.
- If you use more seats than the new plan allows, remove members first.
