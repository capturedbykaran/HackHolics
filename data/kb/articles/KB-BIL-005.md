# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

This article explains how to change your CloudFlow subscription tier. It applies to all customers using product versions 3.x and 4.x. Use the steps below to adjust your seat count or feature set to match your current team requirements.

## Upgrade

If you need additional seats or higher-tier features, you can upgrade your account at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. This means you pay only for the remaining days in your current billing cycle for the difference between your old and new plan rates.

To upgrade your plan:

1.  Log in to your CloudFlow account.
2.  Navigate to the **Account Settings** area.
3.  Select the **Billing** tab.
4.  Click the **Manage Plan** button.
5.  Choose the new plan tier from the list of available options.
6.  Review the prorated charges displayed on the confirmation screen.
7.  Click **Confirm Upgrade** to finalize the change.

Once confirmed, your new limits and features become available instantly. Your next full invoice will reflect the new plan rate starting with the subsequent billing cycle.

## Downgrade

If you need to reduce your seat count or move to a lower-tier plan, you can initiate a downgrade from the billing dashboard. Downgrades take effect at the next billing period. Your current plan remains active until the end of the current paid cycle.

Before you downgrade, ensure your account complies with the limits of the new plan. If you use more seats than the new plan allows, remove members first. You cannot complete the downgrade process while your active seat count exceeds the maximum allowed for the target plan.

To downgrade your plan:

1.  Log in to your CloudFlow account.
2.  Navigate to the **Account Settings** area.
3.  Select the **Billing** tab.
4.  Click the **Manage Plan** button.
5.  Select the desired lower-tier plan.
6.  If prompted, review the list of team members who exceed the new plan's seat limit.
7.  Remove any excess members from the workspace.
8.  Click **Confirm Downgrade** to schedule the change.

After confirmation, you will retain access to your current plan features until the end of the current billing period. At the start of the next billing period, your account will switch to the new plan, and your next invoice will reflect the reduced rate. If you attempt to downgrade without removing excess members, the system will prevent the action until the seat count is within the allowed limit.
