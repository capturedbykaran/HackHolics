# Refund policy (2024)

This policy defines the refund eligibility criteria and approval authority for CloudFlow subscriptions. It applies to all customers using product versions 3.x and 4.x. The rules below are binding and mirrored in the machine-readable policy registry.

## Refund window

A refund request must be submitted within 30 days of the charge date. The 30-day period begins on the specific date the charge was processed. Requests submitted after this 30-day window are not eligible for a refund. This timeframe applies uniformly to all subscription plans associated with product versions 3.x and 4.x. There are no extensions to this deadline. The charge date is the definitive metric for determining eligibility; the date of service initiation or the date of the refund request does not alter the 30-day limit.

## Who approves

Refunds are approved by the billing team. Only the billing team possesses the authority to grant refunds. Customer support agents, automated systems, or other departments cannot approve, deny, or modify refund decisions. The billing team reviews each request against the 30-day window rule stated in the Refund window section. If a request meets the criteria, the billing team executes the refund. If a request fails to meet the criteria, the billing team denies it. No other role within CloudFlow has the permission to override the billing team’s decision or to issue credits outside of this specific approval process. This centralization ensures consistent application of the policy across all product versions 3.x and 4.x.
