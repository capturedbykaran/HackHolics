# Login and password reset problems

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 3.x and 4.x.

## Reset your password

- Click Forgot password on the sign-in page.
- The reset email goes to the registered account email and the link expires after 30 minutes.
- Support can trigger a reset email but never sends links in chat.
