# Inviting team members and managing seats

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 3.x and 4.x.

## Invite people

- Go to Settings > Members and click Invite.
- Only Owners and Admins can invite members.

## Seats

- Seats per plan: Free 1, Pro 5, Business 25, Enterprise 250.
- Invites beyond your seat limit stay pending until a seat frees up or you upgrade.
