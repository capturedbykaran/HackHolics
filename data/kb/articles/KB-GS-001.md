# Creating your CloudFlow account

This article guides new users through the initial setup process for CloudFlow. It is intended for administrators or team leads who need to establish a new tenant and configure the basic workspace settings before inviting colleagues. The instructions apply to product versions 3.x and 4.x.

## Sign up

To begin, navigate to the registration portal. Follow these steps to create your account:

1. Go to app.cloudflow.example in your web browser.
2. Enter your work email address in the designated field.
3. Submit the form to initiate the account creation process.

After submission, you will receive an email containing a verification link. This link expires after 24 hours. If you do not receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder. Once you click the link, your account is activated. Note that every new account starts on the Free plan. You can upgrade or change your plan later from the billing settings if your team grows.

## Set up your workspace

Upon successful verification, you will be prompted to configure your workspace. This step determines how your organization appears within the platform.

1. Choose a workspace name. This name is visible to all members and appears in your workflow URLs. Select a name that is clear and recognizable to your team.
2. Review the default settings. New accounts are created on version 4.3 by default.

The workspace name is critical because it forms part of the unique identifier for your workflows. While you can change this name later in the admin settings, doing so will update the URLs for all existing workflows. Therefore, choose a name that you intend to keep long-term. Once the workspace is named, the system initializes your environment with default permissions and storage limits.

## Next steps

Now that your account and workspace are established, you can begin using CloudFlow. We recommend the following actions to get the most out of the platform:

*   Build your first workflow (KB-GS-002). This guide walks you through creating a simple automation to test your setup.
*   Invite your team (KB-GS-004). Use this article to add colleagues to your workspace and assign appropriate roles.

If you encounter issues during the sign-up process, such as a broken verification link or an error message, contact support with your work email address and the date of the attempt. For questions about plan limits or feature availability on the Free plan, refer to the pricing documentation.
