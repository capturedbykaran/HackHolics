# Creating your CloudFlow account

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 3.x and 4.x.

## Sign up

- Sign up at app.cloudflow.example with a work email.
- Every new account starts on the Free plan.
- Email verification link expires after 24 hours.

## Set up your workspace

- Choose a workspace name; it appears in your workflow URLs.
- New accounts are created on version 4.3.
