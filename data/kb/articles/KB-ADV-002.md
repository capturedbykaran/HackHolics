# Branching and loops

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 4.x.

## Branch

- Branch steps route data on conditions; up to 5 branches per step.

## Loop

- Loop steps iterate over a list of up to 1,000 items.
- Each iteration counts toward the step timeout.
