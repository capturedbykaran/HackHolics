# Invoices and receipts

This article helps CloudFlow users on versions 3.x and 4.x understand how to access their billing documents and interpret the status of their invoices. It covers the process for downloading past statements and explains the different states an invoice can be in within your account.

## Download invoices

You can access all historical billing records from your account settings. To retrieve a specific document, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your CloudFlow account.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
3. Select the invoice you wish to view or download.

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. The currency used for these documents is either USD or INR, depending on your billing configuration. Ensure you have the correct file format saved for your records, as these documents serve as the official record of charges for that billing cycle.

## Invoice statuses

Each invoice in your billing history displays a specific status that indicates the current state of the transaction. The possible statuses are:

*   **paid**: The transaction was successful, and the amount has been collected.
*   **failed**: The payment attempt did not succeed. Check your payment method and retry if necessary.
*   **refunded**: The payment was processed but subsequently returned to the customer.

When an invoice is marked as refunded, the interface displays the specific refund date. This date indicates when the funds were returned to your original payment method. Reviewing these statuses helps you verify that your billing history matches your bank or credit card statements. If you see a failed status, update your payment details in the billing section to prevent service interruptions. For refunded invoices, the refund date provides a clear timeline for accounting purposes.

These statuses apply to all invoices generated in versions 3.x and 4.x. Maintaining an accurate record of these documents is essential for financial auditing and expense reporting. If you have questions about a specific transaction, refer to the invoice number listed in the document header when contacting support.
