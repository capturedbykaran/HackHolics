# Invoices and receipts

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 3.x and 4.x.

## Download invoices

- Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices.
- Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month in USD or INR.

## Invoice statuses

- paid, failed or refunded.
- A refunded invoice shows the refund date.
