# Duplicate charges

This article is for CloudFlow customers on version 3.x and 4.x.

## Why it happens

- A duplicate charge usually comes from a retried payment that succeeded twice.

## What we do

- Duplicate charges are refunded in full once the billing team verifies them (KB-POL-001).
- Support cannot issue refunds directly; the request is handed to billing.
- Billing responds within 1 business day.
