# Duplicate charges

This article explains why duplicate charges occur on your CloudFlow account and outlines the process for resolving them. It applies to users of CloudFlow versions 3.x and 4.x. If you notice two identical charges on your statement, this guide details the cause and the steps our team takes to correct your account.

## Why it happens

A duplicate charge usually comes from a retried payment that succeeded twice. This is a common occurrence in online payment processing. When a payment gateway experiences a temporary timeout or connection issue, the system may automatically retry the transaction. If the original transaction was actually processed successfully but the confirmation signal was lost, the retry results in a second, identical charge. This is not an error in your CloudFlow subscription status, but rather a technical artifact of the payment network. You do not need to cancel your subscription or contact your bank immediately, as the issue is resolved through our internal billing procedures.

## What we do

Once you report a duplicate charge, our team follows a specific protocol to ensure your account is corrected accurately. Please note that Support cannot issue refunds directly; the request is handed to billing. The process works as follows:

1.  Submit a support ticket describing the duplicate charge. Include the date and amount of the transactions.
2.  Our Support team verifies that the charges are indeed duplicates and not separate billing cycles.
3.  The request is forwarded to the billing team for final verification.
4.  Duplicate charges are refunded in full once the billing team verifies them (KB-POL-001).
5.  Billing responds within 1 business day.

After the billing team confirms the duplicate, the refund is initiated. The time it takes for the refund to appear on your bank statement depends on your financial institution and may take several business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the refund is processed. If you do not receive a response within the stated timeframe, please reply to your original support ticket with the ticket number for follow-up. We aim to resolve these issues quickly to minimize any inconvenience to your account.
